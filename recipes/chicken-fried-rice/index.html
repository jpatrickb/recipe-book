Chicken Fried Rice

Mains 🍴 Serves 2-3
asianchickenquickstovetop

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts, diced into 1/2 inch pieces
  • 2 tablespoons oil (peanut, sesame, vegetable, or canola)
  • 1/2 cup white onion, finely chopped
  • 1 cup frozen peas and diced carrots blend
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 3 cups cooked white long grain rice
  • 3-4 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper, optional
  • 1-2 tablespoons sriracha, optional
  • Green onions for garnish, optional

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat a large non-stick wok or skillet to medium-high heat. Add the diced chicken and 2 tbsp oil; sauté for 4-5 minutes until cooked through. Remove chicken from the skillet and set aside.

  2. 2

    In the same skillet, add the frozen peas and carrots blend, and chopped onion. Sauté for 2-3 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add 2 cloves minced garlic and cook for 1 more minute.

  4. 4

    Push the vegetables to one side of the skillet and pour the 2 lightly beaten eggs on the other side; scramble them together with the vegetables.

  5. 5

    Return the cooked chicken to the skillet along with 3 cups cooked rice and 3-4 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce. Stir-fry for another 2-3 minutes.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust seasonings (salt, pepper) as needed. Add 1-2 tbsp sriracha for spice if desired.

  7. 7

    Serve hot, optionally garnished with green onions.

💡 Notes

  • Source: Gimme Delicious (https://gimmedelicious.com/chicken-fried-rice/)
  • Excellent for using leftover rice.
  • Keeps in an airtight container for up to 4 days in the refrigerator or freezes for up to 4 months.
