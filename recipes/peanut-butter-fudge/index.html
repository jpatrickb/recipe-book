Peanut Butter Fudge

Desserts
americanno-bakequickvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (2 sticks) butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 c smooth peanut butter
  • 1 lb powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine butter and peanut butter in a saucepan. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until melted. Add vanilla and powdered sugar and stir to combine.

  2. 2

    Spread into a buttered 8x8 pan lined with parchment paper. Fold excess parchment over the surface and refrigerate until firm. Cut into 1-inch pieces. Store at room temperature in an airtight container up to 1 week.
