Baked Parmesan Crusted Chicken

Mains
chickenitalianbaked

Ingredients

  • Chicken breasts or thighs
  • 3/4 c butter
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 c bread crumbs
  • 1/3 c Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 Tbsp chopped fresh thyme
  • 2 Tbsp chopped fresh basil
  • 2 Tbsp chopped fresh oregano
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 350°. Melt butter with garlic in a saucepan. Remove from heat and stir in bread crumbs, Parmesan, and herbs.

  2. 2

    Dip each piece of chicken in the garlic butter, then press into the bread crumb mixture. Place in a greased 9x13 baking dish.

  3. 3

    Bake 50 minutes until golden and cooked through.
