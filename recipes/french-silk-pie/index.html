French Silk Pie

Desserts
americanchocolateno-bakevegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup butter (not margarine)
  • 3 oz unsweetened chocolate, melted and cooled
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 baked pastry shell
  • Whipped cream and chocolate curls to garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Beat sugar and butter with an electric mixer on medium speed about 4 minutes until fluffy. Stir in melted chocolate and vanilla.

  2. 2

    Add eggs one at a time, beating on high speed after each addition and scraping sides of bowl constantly.

  3. 3

    Transfer to baked pastry shell. Cover and chill 5–24 hours until set. Top with whipped cream and chocolate curls before serving.
