Oatmeal with Toppings

Breakfast 🍴 Serves 1
quickvegetarianstovetop

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup quick oats (dry)
  • 1 cup milk (or water if milk is unavailable)
  • Salt, pinch

Toppings (choose your own)

  • Brown sugar
  • Butter
  • Cinnamon
  • Berries
  • Nuts
  • Peanut butter
  • Honey
  • Jam

Instructions

  1. 1

    Pour 1 cup of milk (or water) into a pot and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.

  2. 2

    Stir in 1/2 cup quick oats and a pinch of salt. Reduce heat to low.

  3. 3

    Simmer for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until oats reach your desired consistency (add more milk if you prefer thinner oatmeal, or simmer longer for thicker).

  4. 4

    Transfer to a bowl.

  5. 5

    Top with your choice of brown sugar, butter, cinnamon, fresh or frozen berries, nuts, peanut butter, honey, or jam.

  6. 6

    Serve warm. Let it cool slightly if needed before eating.

💡 Notes

  • Quick, customizable, and great for using pantry staples. Can scale up easily for multiple servings.
