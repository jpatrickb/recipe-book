Beef Fajitas

Mains
beefmexicanstovetop

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef strips
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2–3 bell peppers, sliced (any color)
  • 2 tomatoes, cut into thin wedges
  • 1 package taco or fajita seasoning
  • Flour tortillas
  • Sour cream
  • Salsa
  • Shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. 1

    In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook beef and onion in 2 Tbsp oil until meat is browned. Add bell peppers and cook several more minutes until crisp-tender.

  2. 2

    Add tomatoes and taco seasoning combined with 1/4 c water. Cook just until water is gone. Serve on tortillas topped with sour cream, salsa, and cheese.
