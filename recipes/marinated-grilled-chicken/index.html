Marinated Grilled Chicken (Xango Chicken)

Mains 🍴 Serves 4–6
chickenasiangrilled

Ingredients

  • 2–3 lbs boneless skinless chicken tenders (thighs taste best)
  • 3 c soy sauce
  • 1 c sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground ginger (or shredded fresh ginger)
  • 1/3 c green onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. 1

    Mix all ingredients together and let chicken marinate for up to 24 hours.

  2. 2

    Grill on an indoor or outdoor grill until no longer pink. Serves 4–6.
