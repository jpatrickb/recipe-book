Fresh Peach Pie

Desserts
americanfruitvegetarianno-bake

Ingredients

  • 8 large fresh peaches, peeled and sliced
  • 1 (9 inch) graham cracker pie crust
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch

Instructions

  1. 1

    In a small saucepan, mix sugar, water, and cornstarch until dissolved. Heat over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture boils and thickens. Remove from heat and cool completely — glaze will thicken more as it cools.

  2. 2

    Place peeled, sliced peaches in a large bowl. Pour half the glaze over the fruit and gently mix to coat. Add more glaze as needed.

  3. 3

    Pour glazed peaches into the pie crust, piling as high as desired. Refrigerate several hours or overnight. Serve cold with whipped cream.
