Baked Hasselback Potatoes

Sides & Salads 🍴 Serves 4-6
vegetarianbakedmake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 5-6 medium potatoes (Russet or Yukon Gold, about 8 oz each)
  • 1.5 tsp extra virgin olive oil (for initial rub)
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt (for initial rub)
  • 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil (for basting)
  • 1.25 tsp kosher salt (for basting)
  • 4 garlic cloves, skin on, smashed, optional but recommended
  • 3 sprigs fresh rosemary, optional but recommended
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 200°C/400°F (180°C fan).

  2. 2

    Scrub and rinse potatoes thoroughly under running water. Pat dry completely with paper towels.

  3. 3

    Place a potato on a cutting board between two wooden spatulas, chopsticks, or wooden skewers that are no thicker than 1cm. These will prevent you from cutting all the way through the potato. Using a sharp knife, slice thinly across the potato (aim for about 2mm slices) perpendicular to the chopsticks, slicing approximately 90% of the way through but not all the way to the bottom. This creates thin "pages" within the potato that will fan out as it cooks.

  4. 4

    Repeat slicing for all potatoes.

  5. 5

    Rub each sliced potato with 1.5 tsp olive oil and sprinkle with 1/4 tsp salt, rubbing the oil and salt into the slices.

  6. 6

    Place potatoes on a roasting tray lined with foil or parchment paper.

  7. 7

    Bake at 200°C/400°F for 30 minutes.

  8. 8

    Remove from oven. Pour 1/4 cup olive oil over the potatoes and sprinkle with 1.25 tsp salt. Scatter smashed garlic cloves and rosemary sprigs around and between the potatoes.

  9. 9

    Return to oven and bake for another 40 minutes, basting generously with the oil from the tray every 10 minutes. The oil helps the slices get crispy and cook evenly.

  10. 10

    After 40 minutes, check that the potatoes are tender inside by piercing with a fork.

  11. 11

    Once soft, increase oven temperature to 220°C/425°F (200°C fan). Baste again and bake for another 10-15 minutes until the edges and top are golden and crispy.

  12. 12

    Remove from oven and serve immediately while hot and crispy. Discard garlic skins if desired.

💡 Notes

  • Source: RecipeTin Eats (https://www.recipetineats.com/hasselback-potatoes/)
  • The key to perfect hasselback potatoes is frequent basting—this creates the crispy exterior while keeping the inside tender.
  • The thin slicing is essential for creating that beautiful fanned effect. Don't skip the garlic and rosemary; they add wonderful flavor.
  • Excellent as a side dish or light main course.
