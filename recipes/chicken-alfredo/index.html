Chicken Alfredo

Mains
chickenitalianpastastovetop

Ingredients

  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 1/2 c milk
  • 3/4 c Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 can chicken breast meat
  • 1 c frozen peas
  • 8 oz fettuccine, cooked

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat cream cheese and milk over medium heat to a boil, stirring constantly. Stir in 1/2 c Parmesan. Reduce heat to low and cook 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add chicken, peas, and pasta to the pot. Toss to combine, top with remaining Parmesan, and serve.

💡 Notes

  • For a more traditional sauce, substitute 2 Tbsp butter and 1 1/2 c heavy cream for the cream cheese and milk.
