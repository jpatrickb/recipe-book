Pumpkin Pancakes with Buttermilk Syrup

Breakfast
stovetopvegetarianfall

Ingredients

Pancakes

  • 1 1/2 c milk
  • 1 c canned pumpkin
  • 1/4 c oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 c flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Pinch of salt

Buttermilk Syrup

  • 1/2 c butter
  • 1 c sugar
  • 1 c buttermilk
  • 1 Tbsp corn syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda

Instructions

  1. 1

    For pancakes: Whisk together milk, pumpkin, oil, and eggs. Add flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt and stir until just combined. Cook on a greased griddle or skillet over medium heat.

  2. 2

    For syrup: Combine butter, sugar, buttermilk, and corn syrup in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring frequently. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla extract and baking soda — it will foam up. Serve warm over pancakes.
