Cafe Rio Cilantro Lime Ranch

Sauces & Seasonings
mexicansaucedressingno-cookquickvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 packet dry ranch dressing mix
  • Buttermilk and mayonnaise (as directed on package)
  • 1 bunch fresh cilantro
  • Tabasco sauce to taste
  • Lime juice to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Prepare ranch dressing according to package directions. Add cilantro, Tabasco, and a squeeze of lime juice.

  2. 2

    Blend or process until smooth and cilantro is fully incorporated. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
