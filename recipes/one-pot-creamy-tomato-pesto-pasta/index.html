One-Pot Creamy Tomato Pesto Pasta

Mains
pastavegetarianstovetopquickone-pot

Ingredients

  • 10 oz dry mafaldine pasta (spaghetti, linguine, or fettuccine also work)
  • 1/3 red onion, diced
  • 2 cups baby spinach, long stems trimmed
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/3 cup tomato sauce
  • 5 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp basil pesto
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream

Finish

  • 2 oz Parmesan cheese, freshly shredded
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced

Instructions

  1. 1

    Layer the dry pasta, onion, spinach, cherry tomatoes, tomato sauce, garlic, pesto, seasonings, butter, and olive oil in a deep skillet, spreading evenly.

  2. 2

    Pour in the chicken stock and heavy cream so the pasta is mostly surrounded by liquid.

  3. 3

    Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat, then cover and reduce to a gentle simmer.

  4. 4

    At 8–9 minutes, lift the lid and press softened noodles down into the liquid; re-cover and simmer until tender, 15–20 minutes total. Add a splash of warm stock if the pan looks dry.

  5. 5

    Off the heat, toss with the lemon juice and Parmesan until melted and glossy. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan if desired.

💡 Notes

  • Pasta substitutions may change cooking time and liquid needs.
  • Don't skip the halfway check — it keeps exposed noodles cooking evenly.
  • The sauce should still look slightly loose when the lid comes off; it thickens as it rests and as the Parmesan goes in.
  • Pairs with air-fried chicken tenderloins, grilled chicken, steak, ground beef, or Italian sausage.
