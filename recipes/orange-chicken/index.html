Orange Chicken

Mains 🍴 Serves 6
chickenasianbakedrice

Ingredients

Main

  • 12 breaded chicken tenders, baked
  • 5 cups cooked rice

Orange Sauce

  • 1 1/2 c water
  • 1 c brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic salt
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 Tbsp frozen orange juice concentrate
  • 1/3 c white vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. 1

    Bake chicken tenders according to package directions.

  2. 2

    Combine all sauce ingredients in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until sauce is thick and bubbly.

  3. 3

    Serve chicken and sauce over rice.
