Creamy Tomato Conchiglie Pasta

Mains 🍴 Serves 4-5
pastavegetarianmake-aheadstovetop

Ingredients

  • 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 package (16 oz) conchiglie (shell pasta)
  • 1 cup whipping cream (or omit for lighter version)
  • Salt to taste
  • Parmesan cheese for serving

Instructions

  1. 1

    Sauté chopped onion in a large pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until translucent.

  2. 2

    Add chopped carrots to the pan and continue sautéing for another 5-7 minutes until the onions are soft and carrots begin to soften.

  3. 3

    Add the can of diced tomatoes (with juices) to the pan and stir to combine.

  4. 4

    Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened slightly and the carrots are tender.

  5. 5

    Season with salt to taste.

  6. 6

    While the sauce simmers, cook the conchiglie pasta according to package directions (typically 8-10 minutes). Drain pasta and set aside.

  7. 7

    If using whipping cream, stir it into the tomato sauce in the last 2 minutes of simmering. Stir well to combine.

  8. 8

    Add the cooked pasta to the tomato mixture and stir gently to coat all shells evenly.

  9. 9

    Transfer to serving bowls or meal-prep containers and sprinkle generously with parmesan cheese.

  10. 10

    Serve warm. Store leftovers in sealed containers in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.

💡 Notes

  • Comfort food classic. Excellent for meal prep.
  • Can be made lighter by omitting cream. Carrots add natural sweetness and nutrition.
