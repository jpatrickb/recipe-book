Spinach Salad

Sides & Salads
americansalad

Ingredients

Salad

  • 8 c fresh spinach
  • 1 c celery, diced
  • 4–5 strips bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • 1 can mandarin oranges, drained

Dressing

  • 1/2 c oil
  • 2 Tbsp vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 Tbsp soy sauce
  • Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine spinach, celery, bacon, eggs, and mandarin oranges in a large bowl.

  2. 2

    Whisk together all dressing ingredients. Toss with salad just before serving.
