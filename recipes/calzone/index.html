Calzone

Mains 🍴 Serves 4
italianbaked

Ingredients

  • Dough for 1 loaf of bread (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 can spaghetti sauce
  • 1 lb mozzarella cheese, grated
  • Meat of choice: pepperoni, sausage, ham, or ground beef

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 375°. Divide dough into 4 pieces. Roll each piece into a circle about 10 inches in diameter.

  2. 2

    Fill each circle with meat, cheese, and spaghetti sauce. Fold in half and pinch edges closed. Place on a greased baking sheet.

  3. 3

    Bake about 20 minutes or until golden brown.
