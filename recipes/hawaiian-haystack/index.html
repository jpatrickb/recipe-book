Hawaiian Haystack

Mains 🍴 Serves 4
chickenamericanricestovetop

Ingredients

Base

  • 1 can chicken, or 2 chicken breasts cooked & shredded
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup
  • 1 can of milk (use empty soup can to measure)
  • 5 c cooked rice

Toppings (choose your favorites)

  • Grated cheese
  • Chopped celery
  • Chopped tomato
  • Chopped green onion
  • Chow mein noodles
  • Pineapple tidbits
  • Coconut flakes

Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine cream of chicken soup, milk, and chicken in a saucepan. Heat over medium heat until warm and bubbly.

  2. 2

    Serve over rice with desired toppings.
