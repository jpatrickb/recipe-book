Indian Butter Chicken

Mains
chickenindianstovetop

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 4 oz plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Sauce

  • 15 oz canned tomato sauce
  • 2 cups whipping cream
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne

Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine marinade ingredients in a large zip-lock bag. Add chicken cubes and let chill for at least 1 hour.

  2. 2

    Add the chicken along with the marinade to a large pot over medium-high heat. Cook about 5 minutes. Add butter and garlic and cook until butter is melted.

  3. 3

    Add tomato sauce, cream, and spices. Stir to combine, then simmer 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Serve with rice and naan bread.
