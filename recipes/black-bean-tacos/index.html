Black Bean Tacos

Mains 🍴 Serves 2
mexicanvegetarianquick

Ingredients

  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 4 flour or corn tortillas
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Mexican blend, or cotija)
  • 1/2 cup salsa (fresh or jarred)
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes (fresh or canned, drained)
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: 1/2 teaspoon cumin or 1/4 teaspoon chili powder

Instructions

  1. 1

    Drain and rinse 1 can of black beans in a colander.

  2. 2

    Heat the beans in a saucepan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until warmed through.

  3. 3

    Season the warm beans with salt, pepper, and optional cumin or chili powder to taste (about 1/2 tsp cumin or 1/4 tsp chili powder total).

  4. 4

    Warm tortillas using one of these methods: directly over a gas flame for 10-15 seconds per side until lightly charred, in a dry skillet over medium heat for 20-30 seconds per side until soft and warm, or wrapped in a damp paper towel and microwaved for 30 seconds.

  5. 5

    Place approximately 1/4 cup warm beans in the center of each tortilla.

  6. 6

    Add toppings: diced tomatoes, 2 tbsp salsa, cheese, sliced avocado, or any other preferred toppings.

  7. 7

    Fold tortillas and serve immediately.

💡 Notes

  • Budget-friendly, quick, and very flexible with toppings.
  • Extra beans can be refrigerated for 4 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
