Queso Mac & Cheese

Mains
chickenmexicanpastaslow-cooker

Ingredients

Chicken & Vegetable Mix

  • 2 orange bell peppers
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 onion (yellow or white)
  • 2 lbs chicken breast, cubed
  • 1/2 can red enchilada sauce
  • 1 can green chiles
  • 1 can fire-roasted tomatoes
  • 2 Tbsp garlic purée
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

Queso Sauce

  • 1 1/2 lbs shell pasta
  • 3 cups cottage cheese
  • 4 oz plain cream cheese
  • 6 oz extra sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 can red enchilada sauce
  • 3/4 cup milk

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 350°. Dice the bell peppers and onion, spread on a greased baking sheet, and roast 30–40 minutes.

  2. 2

    Combine roasted vegetables with chicken, enchilada sauce, green chiles, fire-roasted tomatoes, garlic, onion powder, salt, pepper, and cayenne in a slow cooker. Cook on low 3–4 hours.

  3. 3

    Cook shell pasta for half the time shown on the box. Blend together the queso sauce ingredients (cottage cheese, cream cheese, cheddar, enchilada sauce, milk) until smooth.

  4. 4

    Add the par-cooked pasta and blended queso sauce to the slow cooker with the chicken and vegetables. Cook on high 15–20 minutes until pasta is tender.
