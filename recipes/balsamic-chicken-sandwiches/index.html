Balsamic Chicken Sandwiches

Mains 🍴 Serves 6
chickenitaliangrilledsandwich

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1 bottle balsamic vinaigrette
  • 6 sub buns, ciabatta rolls, or 2 baguettes
  • 1 lb fresh mozzarella, thinly sliced
  • 6 Tbsp pesto
  • 3 tomatoes, thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. 1

    Marinate chicken in balsamic vinaigrette overnight. Grill chicken until cooked through, then slice into thin strips.

  2. 2

    If using baguettes, cut each into 3 pieces then slice open. Spread one side of each piece with 1 Tbsp pesto. Layer chicken, mozzarella, and tomato slices. Top with the other side of bread.

  3. 3

    Serve immediately, or wrap in foil and heat 5–10 minutes in a 300° oven.
