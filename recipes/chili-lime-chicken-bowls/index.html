Chili-Lime Chicken Bowls

Mains 🍴 Serves 4
mexicanchickenmake-aheadquick

Ingredients

  • 1 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs (4 small pieces)
  • 2 cups sliced bell peppers, optional
  • 4 cups cooked rice of choice

Marinade

  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice (or 1 lime)
  • 2 teaspoons lime zest (or 1 lime)
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced or crushed
  • 1/4 cup cilantro, minced
  • 4 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder, optional
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. 1

    In a large bowl, whisk together all marinade ingredients (lime juice, lime zest, minced garlic, cilantro, 4 tbsp oil, brown sugar, chili powder, cumin powder, salt, and black pepper).

  2. 2

    Add 1 pound chicken (breasts or thighs) to the marinade bowl and mix until fully coated. Marinate for at least 20 minutes (or up to 8 hours for more flavor).

  3. 3

    Heat a grill or skillet to medium-high heat. Cook marinated chicken for 5-8 minutes per side until cooked through. Let cool 5 minutes, then slice.

  4. 4

    Place 2 cups sliced bell peppers in the same pan and cook for 3-4 minutes until charred.

  5. 5

    Allow everything to cool to room temperature.

  6. 6

    Distribute 1 cup cooked rice into each of 4 meal-prep containers, then divide sliced chicken and peppers equally among them.

  7. 7

    Seal containers and refrigerate for up to 4 days.

💡 Notes

  • Source: Gimme Delicious (https://gimmedelicious.com/chili-lime-chicken-and-rice-meal-prep-bowls/)
  • Excellent for meal prep, stays fresh 4 days refrigerated.
  • Can customize with toppings like avocado, sour cream, or black beans.
  • Family favorite — rated 5/5.
