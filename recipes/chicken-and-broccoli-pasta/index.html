Chicken and Broccoli Pasta with White Sauce

Mains 🍴 Serves 3
pastachickenstovetop

Ingredients

  • 250g (8 oz) boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 250g (8 oz) broccoli florets
  • 250g (8 oz) short pasta (penne or rigatoni)
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 tbsp vegetable or olive oil
  • 5-7 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook until al dente according to package directions (typically 8-10 minutes). Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water, then drain pasta and set aside.

  2. 2

    In the same pot of boiling water (or a separate pot), add broccoli florets and steam for 8-10 minutes until tender-crisp. Drain and set aside.

  3. 3

    Pat the chicken breast dry with paper towels and season both sides with salt and pepper.

  4. 4

    Heat 2 tbsp butter and 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Once hot, add chicken breast and sear for 8-10 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (internal temperature should reach 165°F). Remove chicken to a cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes, then slice into bite-sized pieces.

  5. 5

    In the same skillet, heat remaining 1 tbsp butter and 2 tbsp oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant, being careful not to burn.

  6. 6

    Sprinkle flour over the garlic and oil, stirring constantly for about 1-2 minutes until the raw flour smell disappears and it forms a light paste (roux).

  7. 7

    Slowly pour in milk and cream, stirring constantly to avoid lumps. Continue stirring as the sauce heats and thickens, about 3-5 minutes.

  8. 8

    Season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste.

  9. 9

    Add cooked pasta and broccoli to the sauce, stirring gently to coat everything evenly. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of reserved pasta water to reach desired consistency.

  10. 10

    Add sliced chicken back to the pan and gently toss to combine.

  11. 11

    Serve immediately, optionally drizzling with a bit of extra virgin olive oil.

💡 Notes

  • Source: Flavor Quotient (https://flavorquotient.com/2016/11/broccoli-and-chicken-pasta-with-white-sauce/)
  • Quick, creamy, and satisfying pasta dish with good protein from the chicken.
  • The key to a smooth sauce is tempering the milk gradually and stirring constantly.
  • For a lighter version, reduce the cream to 1/4 cup and increase milk to 2 cups.
