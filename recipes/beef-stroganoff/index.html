Beef Stroganoff

Mains 🍴 Serves 4–6
beefamericanpastastovetop

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 1 lb beef, cubed
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup
  • 1/2 c sour cream
  • 1–2 beef bouillon cubes
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 c milk
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Cook pasta according to package directions. Meanwhile, heat 1 Tbsp oil in a large skillet and cook beef until no longer pink.

  2. 2

    In a bowl, combine cream soup, sour cream, milk, and Worcestershire sauce; whisk until smooth. Add to the meat along with bouillon cubes. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until warm and bubbly and bouillon is dissolved.

  3. 3

    Season with salt and pepper. Serve sauce over drained pasta.
