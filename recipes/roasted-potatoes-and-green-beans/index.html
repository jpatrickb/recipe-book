Roasted Potatoes and Green Beans

Sides & Salads 🍴 Serves 2-3
vegetarianbakedquick

Ingredients

For the Potatoes

  • 1½ lbs mini potatoes, sliced in half or quarters
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • A few turns freshly ground black pepper

For the Beans

  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed and cut in half
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt

For Garnish (optional)

  • ⅓ cup freshly grated parmesan cheese
  • Chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

  2. 2

    Toss potato pieces with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, ½ tsp kosher salt, and black pepper to taste. Spread evenly on the prepared baking sheet.

  3. 3

    Roast potatoes for 20 minutes.

  4. 4

    While potatoes are roasting, combine trimmed and halved green beans with 1 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp garlic powder, and ¼ tsp kosher salt in the same bowl used for potatoes.

  5. 5

    After the initial 20 minutes, remove the baking sheet, stir the potatoes, then add the green beans in an even layer around them.

  6. 6

    Roast for another 25 minutes until potatoes are tender inside and beans are fork-tender (total roasting time: 45 minutes).

  7. 7

    Sprinkle with ⅓ cup grated parmesan cheese and chopped fresh parsley if desired.

  8. 8

    Serve immediately. Store leftovers in a sealed container for up to 4 days.

💡 Notes

  • Source: The Recipe Well (https://therecipewell.com/roasted-potatoes-and-green-beans/)
  • Simple, budget-friendly side dish. Can be eaten warm as a side or standalone light meal.
