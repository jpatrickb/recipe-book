Honey Pecan Granola

Breakfast 🍴 Serves 12
bakedmake-aheadvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 cups old-fashioned oats
  • 1 cup pecans, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 cup chia seeds
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup coconut oil, melted
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat oven to 300°F. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment.

  2. 2

    In a large bowl, stir together oats, pecans, chia seeds, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt.

  3. 3

    In a smaller bowl, whisk the melted coconut oil, honey, maple syrup, and vanilla until smooth.

  4. 4

    Pour the wet over the dry and stir until every oat is coated — 30 seconds longer than feels necessary. Dry patches are what turn into loose, dusty granola.

  5. 5

    Spread on the sheet pan and press flat with the back of a spatula. Pressing is what makes clusters.

  6. 6

    Bake 40–45 minutes, rotating the pan once at 20 minutes. Don't stir if you want big clusters; stir every 15 minutes if you'd rather have loose granola. It will still look slightly soft when done — it crisps as it cools.

  7. 7

    Cool completely on the pan, undisturbed, about an hour. Then break into clusters.

💡 Notes

  • Makes about 6 cups (12 servings of 1/2 cup). Prep 10 minutes, bake 45 minutes, cool 1 hour.
  • Store airtight at room temperature for 2–3 weeks, or freeze up to 3 months. The pecans set the limit — their oils go rancid before the granola goes stale.
