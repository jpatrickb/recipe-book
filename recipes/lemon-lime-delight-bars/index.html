Lemon-Lime Delight Bars

Desserts 🍴 Serves ~24 bars
bakeddesserts

Ingredients

Crust

  • 2 sticks butter
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups flour

Filling

  • 4 eggs, beaten
  • 2 cups sugar
  • ¼ cup flour
  • 4 Tbsp. fresh lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp. fresh lime juice & zest to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine butter, powdered sugar, and flour, mixing well. Press into 9×13 baking pan and bake 15 minutes at 350 degrees.

  2. 2

    Blend eggs, sugar, flour, juices, and zest together; pour into slightly cooled crust. Bake at 350 degrees for 25 minutes or until set.

  3. 3

    Chill and sprinkle with additional powdered sugar if desired.

💡 Notes

  • Throw a few of these bars into a blender with some vanilla ice cream. You'll have a refreshing shake with cookie bits!
