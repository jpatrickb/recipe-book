Cheesy Garlic Chicken Wraps

Mains 🍴 Serves 4
chickenquickmake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
  • 5-6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1.5 cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
  • 2-3 green onions (scallions), sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Hot sauce, optional (1-2 tbsp if desired)
  • Oil for pan-frying (about 2 tbsp)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Slice or dice the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, sour cream (or yogurt), minced garlic, and hot sauce if using. Mix until smooth and creamy.

  3. 3

    Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces and cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden and cooked through. Let cool slightly, then roughly chop the cooked chicken.

  4. 4

    Place a tortilla flat on a work surface.

  5. 5

    Spread about 2 tbsp of the mayo-garlic mixture down the center of the tortilla.

  6. 6

    Layer with cooked chicken (about 1/4 of the total), then sprinkle with shredded cheese (about 1/3 cup) and sliced green onions.

  7. 7

    Fold in the sides of the tortilla and roll tightly from one end to create a burrito-style wrap.

  8. 8

    Heat 1 tbsp oil in the same skillet over medium heat. Place wraps seam-side down and pan-fry for 3-4 minutes per side until golden, crispy, and the cheese melts.

  9. 9

    Serve immediately while the cheese is warm and melted.

💡 Notes

  • Source: Cooked and Loved (https://www.cookedandloved.com/recipes/cheesy-garlic-chicken-wraps/)
  • Can be assembled ahead and refrigerated, then cooked fresh before serving for best texture.
  • For a lighter version, use air fryer at 375°F for 6-8 minutes instead of pan-frying.
