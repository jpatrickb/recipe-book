Garbanzo Bean Curry

Mains 🍴 Serves 2-3
indianvegetarianstovetop

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas), drained and rinsed
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, with juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups cooked rice (white, brown, or jasmine)
  • Optional: 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes, chopped cilantro for serving

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat 3 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add diced onion (about 1 medium onion) and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and slightly translucent.

  3. 3

    Add 3 minced garlic cloves and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

  4. 4

    Add 2 tablespoons curry powder and 1 teaspoon ground cumin. Stir constantly for about 1 minute to toast the spices and release their flavors.

  5. 5

    Add drained and rinsed garbanzo beans (1 can) and stir to coat with the curry mixture.

  6. 6

    Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juice and stir to combine well.

  7. 7

    Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat and cook for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  8. 8

    Season with salt and pepper to taste. Taste and add additional curry powder or cumin if desired. Add red pepper flakes for heat if desired.

  9. 9

    If rice is not already cooked, prepare 2 cups of cooked rice according to package directions while the curry simmers.

  10. 10

    Serve curry over approximately 1/2 cup cooked rice per serving in individual bowls. Top with chopped cilantro if desired.

💡 Notes

  • Hearty, flavorful, and vegetarian.
  • Can add 1/2 cup coconut milk in the last 5 minutes for a creamier version, or add 1 cup frozen vegetables along with the beans.
  • Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
