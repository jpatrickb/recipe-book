Alfredo Sauce

Mains 🍴 Serves 4–6
italianpastastovetopquickvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 c heavy cream
  • 1 cube (1/2 c) butter
  • 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • Minced garlic or garlic salt, to taste
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 16 oz pasta, cooked

Instructions

  1. 1

    Over medium heat, bring cream and butter to a boil. Remove from heat, add Parmesan cheese, and stir until melted.

  2. 2

    Season with salt, pepper, and garlic. Serve over 16 oz cooked pasta.
