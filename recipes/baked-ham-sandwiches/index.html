Baked Ham Sandwiches

Mains 🍴 Serves 12
porkamericanbakedsandwichmake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 12 sweet Hawaiian dinner rolls, halved
  • 3/4 pound thinly sliced ham
  • 6 slices cheese
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp poppy seeds

Instructions

  1. 1

    Place the bottoms of the rolls in a baking dish. Top with ham and cheese, cutting to size if necessary.

  2. 2

    Melt butter and stir in Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, and poppy seeds. Brush the butter mixture over the sandwiches, using it all. Place tops on sandwiches and cover with foil. Chill several hours or overnight.

  3. 3

    Bake at 375° for 15 minutes, removing the foil for the last few minutes to brown the tops.
