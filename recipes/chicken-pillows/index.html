Chicken Pillows

Mains 🍴 Serves 2–4
chickenamericanbaked

Ingredients

  • 1 c cubed chicken (canned works fine)
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2 Tbsp fresh chives, chopped
  • Garlic salt, salt & pepper to taste
  • 1 pkg refrigerated crescent rolls
  • 4 Tbsp butter, melted
  • 1/2 c bread crumbs
  • 1 pkg chicken gravy mix, prepared

Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine chicken, cream cheese, chives, and seasonings; stir until just mixed.

  2. 2

    Unroll crescent rolls, pressing 2 together to form rectangles. Place 1/4 of filling on each rectangle, fold over, and pinch edges to seal.

  3. 3

    Dip each pillow in melted butter, then roll in bread crumbs. Bake at 375° for 20 minutes or until golden brown. Serve topped with prepared chicken gravy.
