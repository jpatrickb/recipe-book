Sausage Gnocchi Soup

Mains
soupsausagegnocchistovetopone-pot

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground Italian sausage
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed, drained and chopped
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 2/3 cup dry white wine (or 2/3 cup extra chicken stock + 1 Tbsp lemon juice)
  • 5 cups chicken stock, low-sodium preferred
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 lb potato gnocchi (DeLallo preferred)
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Brown the sausage in a Dutch oven or stockpot over medium-low heat, breaking it into crumbles. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate, leaving about 1 Tbsp of drippings in the pot.

  2. 2

    Cook the onion in the drippings about 5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and cook about 1 minute until fragrant.

  3. 3

    Stir in the seasonings, then the tomato paste and sun-dried tomatoes; cook 1–2 minutes until the paste darkens slightly.

  4. 4

    Sprinkle in the flour and stir about 1 minute until no dry streaks remain.

  5. 5

    Pour in the wine (or the stock-and-lemon swap), scraping up the browned bits; cook 2–3 minutes until slightly reduced.

  6. 6

    Gradually add the stock and cream, return the sausage to the pot, and bring to a boil.

  7. 7

    Add the gnocchi and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until plump and tender — don't overcook.

  8. 8

    Reduce to a gentle simmer; stir in the spinach until wilted, then the Parmesan until melted.

  9. 9

    Salt to taste and serve with extra Parmesan.

💡 Notes

  • Gnocchi go gummy if boiled past tender — pull them at 4–5 minutes.
  • Leftovers thicken as the gnocchi absorb broth; loosen with a splash of stock or cream when reheating.
  • The base can be made ahead; cook the gnocchi close to serving.
