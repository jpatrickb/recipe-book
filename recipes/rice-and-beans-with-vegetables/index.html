Rice & Beans with Vegetables

Mains 🍴 Serves 3-4
vegetarianstovetopmake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (white, brown, or jasmine)
  • 1 (15 oz) can beans (black, pinto, or kidney beans), drained and rinsed
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, with juice
  • 1 cup corn (fresh, frozen, or canned)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: 1/4 teaspoon chili powder or red pepper flakes for heat

Instructions

  1. 1

    If rice is not already cooked, prepare 2 cups of cooked rice according to package directions.

  2. 2

    Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

  3. 3

    Add 3 minced garlic cloves and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.

  4. 4

    Add drained and rinsed beans (1 can) and corn (1 cup). Stir and cook for 2 minutes.

  5. 5

    Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juice and stir to combine.

  6. 6

    Add 1 teaspoon ground cumin and stir well.

  7. 7

    Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  8. 8

    Season with salt and pepper to taste. Add optional chili powder or red pepper flakes if desired.

  9. 9

    Serve over 1/2 cup cooked rice per serving.

💡 Notes

  • Budget-friendly, flexible with vegetables and bean types.
  • Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months. Works well as a meal prep.
