One-Pot French Onion Pasta

Mains 🍴 Serves 3-4
pastavegetarianstovetop

Ingredients

  • 3 large yellow onions, sliced 1/4" thin
  • 12 oz mezze rigatoni or other short pasta
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp fresh thyme, chopped
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 cups low sodium beef broth
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1 cup Gruyère cheese, grated
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. 1

    Melt butter with olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add sliced onions and sauté frequently, stirring often, for about 1 hour until they reach a deep golden brown color and become jammy. This is the key step—patience is essential for developing the rich onion flavor.

  3. 3

    Once caramelized, add minced garlic and fresh thyme, cooking for about 3-4 minutes until very fragrant.

  4. 4

    Pour in white wine and deglaze the pot by scraping up the browned bits from the bottom with a wooden spoon.

  5. 5

    Add Worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper, stirring to combine.

  6. 6

    Add beef broth, water, and uncooked pasta directly to the pot. Stir to combine.

  7. 7

    Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce heat to medium-low and simmer while stirring frequently for about 8-10 minutes, until the pasta is al dente and a good amount of liquid has evaporated (the mixture should be saucy but not soupy).

  8. 8

    Remove from heat and stir in heavy cream and grated Gruyère cheese until fully incorporated and creamy.

  9. 9

    Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. Serve immediately.

💡 Notes

  • Source: Cozy Cravings (https://cozycravings.com/one-pot-french-onion-pasta/)
  • Cozy, one-pot meal inspired by French onion soup. The long caramelization time is crucial for developing depth of flavor.
  • For best results, use a good quality Gruyère cheese.
