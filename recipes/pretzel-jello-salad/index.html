Pretzel Jello Salad

Desserts
americanfruitbakedmake-aheadvegetarian

Ingredients

Pretzel Crust

  • 2 c pretzels, crushed
  • 1/2 c butter, melted
  • 3 Tbsp sugar

Cream Cheese Layer

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 c sugar
  • 8 oz Cool Whip, thawed

Strawberry Jello Layer

  • 6 oz strawberry Jello
  • 2 c boiling water
  • 20 oz frozen strawberries

Instructions

  1. 1

    Mix crushed pretzels, melted butter, and sugar. Press into the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish. Bake at 350° for 8–10 minutes. Let cool.

  2. 2

    Beat cream cheese, sugar, and Cool Whip until smooth. Spread over the cooled crust, making sure to cover all the pretzels and seal the edges completely.

  3. 3

    Dissolve Jello in 2 c boiling water. Stir in frozen strawberries until combined. Pour over the cream cheese layer and chill until completely set.
