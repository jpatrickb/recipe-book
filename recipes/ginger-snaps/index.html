Ginger Snaps

Desserts
americancookiesbakedvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 3/4 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup molasses
  • Extra sugar for rolling

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 350°. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add egg and molasses and mix well. Stir in dry ingredients (flour, ginger, cinnamon, salt, baking soda).

  2. 2

    Form into small balls and roll in sugar. Place on baking sheet and bake 10 minutes. Cool on a rack.
